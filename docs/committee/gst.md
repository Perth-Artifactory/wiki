## Classifications

Transactions fall into one of four categories

* **Incurs GST** - Included in our BAS and incurred GST. Pretty much all supply that does not have an exemption.
* **GST Free** - Included in our BAS but did not attract GST, typically government fees or a non-commercial activity.
* **BAS Excluded** - Do not constitute a supply, not included in our BAS.
* **Input Taxed** - Excluded from BAS because we report using the Simpler BAS format

### Non-commercial supply

Some supplies that would otherwise incur GST can be treated as GST free if they are determined to be a non-commerical supply. For a supply to be non-commercial it needs to be less than 75% of the cost of supply (nothing we currently do) or less than 50% of the market value of the supply. ## Ambiguity

When there is ambiguity in a classification:

* Treat income as incurring GST
* Treat expenses as GST free

This way any classification errors in our reporting will err on the side of **overpaying** and **underclaiming** GST.

Regardless of how obvious a classification is we cannot **claim** GST payments without a tax invoice stating that payment.

### Containers for change

* Other people returning containers and nominating us: **BAS Excluded** donation
* Collection within the space:
  * An attendee placing a container in one of our internal collection bins is **donating** the can to the organisation.
  * We send cans to Good Samaritan Industries. In exchange for 20% of the **cans themselves** (as per our contract with them the actual price we pay for collection is cans not money) Good Samaritan Industries **donates** the value of the rest of the cans to us.
  * Regardless of whether Good Samaritan Industries should be collecting GST on the value of 20% of the cans the net funds we receive back directly from the Containers for Change program is a **BAS excluded** donation.

## Expenses (money out)

### Rent, Electricity, Internet, Waste

**Incurs GST**

### Insurance

While most aspects of our insurance bills **incur GST** check for line items related to stamp duty. Stamp duty is classified as **BAS Excluded**

### Bank account fees, international transaction fees

**Input Taxed** provided the charge is from a bank. Treated as supply of a financial service

### Card processing, direct debit

**Incurs GST**
