## Market

Our primary differentiating factor as a for-hire event space is our permissive usage limits, specifically as they relate to tool usage and allowed noise.

## Locality of supply

Public event attendees are sensitive to location, especially when the event is arts/creative focused.[^1] Options that were not accessible by public transport or were more than 10km from the CBD were excluded.

[^1]: [Johnson, V., Currie, G., & Stanley, J. (2011). Exploring transport to arts and cultural activities as a facilitator of social inclusion. Transport Policy, Vol. 18, (1), pp. 68-75.](https://www.worldtransitresearch.info/research/3862/)

## Quality/nature of supply

Comparisons considered the facilities provided by the space. In particular the following amenities were considered a positive:

* Capacity (vs our 50-100)
* Onsite parking (vs yes)
* Tables (vs workspace for 40)
* Kitchenette or preferrably a full kitchen (vs full kitchen)
* Toilet facilities (vs yes)
* AV (vs sound, presentation, lighting)
* Ability to clean the space after an event (vs easy)
* Onsite concessions (vs yes)
* Air conditioning (vs yes)
* Power (Where supply is suitable for tool usage) (vs yes)

## Size, quantity, or duration of supply

Rather than pricing per hour (most common) or a flat rate per day our pricing is typically per head. We have used this difference to calculate a break point in terms of both attendees and rental time.
