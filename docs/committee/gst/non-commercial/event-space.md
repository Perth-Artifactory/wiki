## Market

Our primary differentiating factor as a for-hire event space is our permissive usage limits, specifically as they relate to tool usage and allowed noise.

## Locality of supply

Public event attendees are sensitive to location, especially when the event is arts/creative focused.[^1] Options that were not accessible by public transport or were more than 10km from the CBD were excluded.

[^1]: [Johnson, V., Currie, G., & Stanley, J. (2011). Exploring transport to arts and cultural activities as a facilitator of social inclusion. Transport Policy, Vol. 18, (1), pp. 68-75.](https://www.worldtransitresearch.info/research/3862/)

## Quality/nature of supply

Comparisons considered the facilities provided by the space. In particular the following amenities were considered a positive:

* ~~Capacity (vs our 50-100)~~ See: Size, quantity, or duration of supply
* Onsite parking (vs yes)
* Tables (vs workspace for 40)
* Kitchenette or preferrably a full kitchen (vs full kitchen)
* AV (vs sound, presentation, lighting)
* Ability to clean the space after an event (vs easy)
* Onsite concessions (vs yes)
* Air conditioning (vs yes)
* Power (Where supply is suitable for tool usage) (vs yes)

## Size, quantity, or duration of supply

Rather than pricing per hour (most common) or a flat rate per day our pricing is typically per head. If we attempted to take an average $/h it would not accurately reflect the size of either venue being compared. Instead our methodology is to adjust[^2] the per hour pricing based on amenities provided then set the market rate as the adjusted rate charged by the cheapest alternative that can accomodate the event in question.

[^2]: [In a case where there are genuinely identifiable differences in the quality of the 'same supply', the charity needs to take into account those differences in establishing the market value of the supply it makes. ## Amenities adjustment

* -$x indicates that the price of an alternative was reduced because they have an amenity we lack.
* +$x indicates that the price has been increased for a better amenity we have.
* † indicates that while the Artifactory represents better value for money for a particular comparison quantifying that value represents a significant compliance cost and the benefit has been discarded.
* ‡ indicates a potential exclusionary criteria. That is, the venue is incompatible with a particular event type

Adjustments are applied on a case by case basis per event type
