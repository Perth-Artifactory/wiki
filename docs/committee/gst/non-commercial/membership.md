## Market

Apart from the range of facilities offered by the space the primary differentiating factor between us and a random paid workshop is 24/7 access. We have erred on the side of caution and compared businesses that provide 24/7 access to **at least one** of the discplines we provide. There are no comparible businesses that provide access to all Artifactory facilities.

## Locality of supply

We explored options within the Perth metropolitan region

## Quality/nature of supply

In all instances the capabilities of our facilities are either the same or better. (Larger equipment etc)

## Size, quantity, or duration of supply

We work on a month-to-month basis whereas some operators are week-to-week. As per ATO advice we have multiplied the weekly price for one comparison to achieve an approximate monthly price. In part this was done because the computed monthly price roughly sat in the middle of other comparisons.

## Condition of supply

Comparisons were restricted to operators that provide **unlimited** 24/7 access. Options where access was 24/7 but limited to a certain number of visits are a meaningful difference in condition of supply and were excluded.

## CBD coworking scaling

* Identify the 'same' supply and its price: See above
* Identify all the differing characteristics shown in the quality of the 'same supply' when comparing with the supply the charity makes (format: them vs us)
  * Location (CBD (business distance) vs Osborne Park (supplier distance, freeway))
  * Parking (None vs provided)
  * Kitchen facilities (Kitchenette vs full kitchen)
  * Maker facilities (Limited printing/desktop laser vs full industrial)
  * Mentoring (Not meaningfully provided vs consultant level)
  * Business networking (Meaningful vs largely not present)
  * Aesthetic (Modern vs industrial)
  * Meeting rooms (Dedicated vs dependant on usage)
  * Noise restrictions (Primarily office vs industrial zoning)
* Quantify those differing characteristics on a reasonable basis, and
  * Location: While there are obviously differences each is more appropriate for their intended usage. Conclusion is: **even**
  * Parking: [CPP Parking](https://www.cityofperthparking.com.au/parking-fees) has a limit of $15/day, other options are more expensive. Assuming 3 days a week (likely under estimation in their favour) is $180/month in parking charges. Conclusion is: **+$180** in cost for other options
  * Kitchen facilities: A kitchenette is unable to prepare full meals but the compliance cost to quantify the benefit of ingredient cost vs purchased meals is significant. Conclusion is: **Shift would benefit Artifactory and thus can be ignored**
  * Maker facilities: Compliance cost in quantification is significant. Conclusion is: **Shift would heavily benefit Artifactory and thus can be ignored**
  * Mentoring: Quantifying cost would undermine competitive confidentiality of otherwise commercial offerings provided by volunteers. Conclusion is: **Shift would heavily benefit Artifactory and thus can be ignored**
  * Business networking: Very difficult to quantify. On balance we believe that the unquantified advantage provided by our maker facilities and mentoring would outweigh the value of business networking. Conclusion is: **Equal**
  * Aesthetic: Difficult to quantify due to differing taste of customers. An explicit difference is the lack of greenery at the Artifactory. This can be somewhat quantified as a 15%[^1] increase in productivity. Conclusion is: Flux/WeWork price should be reduced by 15% to account for productivity increase
  * Meeting rooms: If we assume that the Artifactory has no meeting spaces (erring on the side of caution) then all meetings would need to be conducted offsite. Spacecube offers 5 1hr meeting sessions per month. A [somewhat equivalent](https://www.stirling.wa.gov.au/stirling-leisure/venue-and-facility-hire/meeting-room-stirling-libraries-inglewood) space within close proximity to the Artifactory would cost $26-43/hr (non commercial vs commercial). This averages to $34.50/hour or $172.50 per month. Conclusion is: **Coworking spaces provide an additional value of ~$172.50 per month
  * Noise restrictions: Compliance cost in quantification is significant. Conclusion is: **Shift would heavily benefit Artifactory and thus can be ignored**
* Use the quantified values of those differing characteristics to adjust the price of the 'same supply'.
  * Spacecubed:
    * +$495 - Starting
    * -$73.80 (15%) - Productivity increase from plants
    * +$180 - Parking costs
    * -$172.50 - External meeting room costs
    * Final: $428.70
  * WeWork
    * +$357.50 - Starting
    * -$53.63 (15%) - Productivity increase from plants
    * +$180 - Parking costs
    * -$172.50 - External meeting room costs
    * Final: $311.37

[^1]: [Employees working in environments with plants experienced a 15% increase in productivity](https://indoorplantsolutions.com.au/the-roi-of-greenery-how-plants-enhance-employee-wellbeing-and-productivity/#Tangible_Benefits_of_Office_Plants:~:text=employees%20working%20in%20environments%20with%20plants%20experienced%20a%2015%25%20increase%20in%20productivity)
