## Background (informative)

There are many past contributors who have dropped out of attendance, because their lives are currently too busy to participate in the Artifactory community (or, indeed, any community group.)

Each year, we'd like to recognise a few of these people as Friends of the Artifactory. This status would let them attend social events (without paying the non-member event pricing), so they can stay connected to our community.

This aligns with our mission to '...prevent or relieve social isolation' by encouraging people to remain connected to our community, even if they don't currently have the capacity to participate as regular members.

## Benefits

Being a Friend of the Artifactory confers the following benefits:

* Can attend members-only social events at the Artifactory.
* Pays the member price to attend social events (e.g. does not have to pay the Day Pass rate.)

Being a Friend of the Artifactory does not confer any other benefit of membership.

## Appointments

Friends of the Artifactory must:

* Have made a significant contribution to the Artifactory.
* Have been a member at some time within the last 5 years.

Friends of the Artifactory can be appointed in one of two ways.

* By a resolution at a general meeting.
* By a resolution of the committee.

Appointments expire on the day of the next annual general meeting after the appointment is made.

Appointments can also be terminated by the committee if the person acts against the interests of the association.

## Notes on design (informative)

* The status of "Friend of the Artifactory" should usually be conferred at an annual general meeting, by a vote of the Artifactory community. (Nominations will be called for as part of the AGM notice.)
* The committee only expects to appoint people as "Friend of the Artifactory" for recently resigned members, between AGMs.
* The benefit is keeping people connected to our community.
* The cost is forgone Day Pass fees that the person would have paid, if they had attended the social events in question. We expect this to be a negligible amount of revenue over the course of the year.
