### Membership fee review

Our membership fees have not increased for 12 years, and are not sufficient to continue supporting our operations. I believe that our absolute minimum aim should be for our membership fees to cover our lease and our utility costs. This would still rely on fundraising and other income to cover our other operating costs (cleaning materials, repairs, consumables) as well as any capital expenses.

Last year our membership fees brought in $72,490, while our lease and utilities were $77,665.

We need to increase our fees, while minimising any resulting membership cancellations. I believe our inactive members/supporters are most at risk here.

My proposal is to increase our fees from $75/45 to $85/50, while grandfathering current members to the current rate while they retain their current membership. The higher rate would apply to new or rejoining members, and could potentially have a start date of 1st January 2027 to try and drive membership joining in the next few months.

Last year, 53 members maintained their membership for the whole 12 months, which is about half our average membership. 

My back of the envelope calculations are that this action would bring in an extra $3,000 next year, growing slightly each year. 

Legacy members would also be encouraged to increase their monthly payments by adding a regular tax deductible donation.

Had we done this last year, we still would likely have fallen short of our target, however I'm not sure there is appetite to raise it any further at this time. I do think it likely we will need to make a similar incremental increase in following years to remain financially viable under our current operational model.
