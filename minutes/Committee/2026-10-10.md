## Treasurer's Report

The Treasurer's Disclaimer: The numbers below have been calculated in a quick way and have not undergone robust checking as for an Annual Report.

Good news in that we have received 2 grants! $2,600 for portable aircons will be shown in October figures, the $3,500 shown here is for digital items to support our volunteers, such as cameras for eaasier creation of promotional images.

A few people have already adjusted their membership fees to include a regular donation. Further promotion/reminders over time will probably be beneficial.

Timely payment of membership invoices is still an issue and taking up significant volunteer time each month. I've discussed with Fletcher putting a flag for training requests where invoices are overdue. We will also look at introducing a checklist in new member inductions which will include a check whether the first invoice has been paid. This has become possible due to all the work Fletcher has put into the portal, along with the switch to Xero. Membership payments can easily be reconciled daily while I'm eating breakfast

Financial reports are looking a little different this year, as figures are all exclusive of GST. While this will come closer to representing correct figures once we submit our BAS and receive the expected refund, it won't line up with the ongoing bank balances. We will need to spend some time checking through the accounts to make sure the data and our estimations are correct before we do the registration and submit our BAS.

Talkshop income has come in fairly short the first 3 months ($500). About half of this is an expected effect of GST where we are charging GST on food, but not paying it on the cost of ingredients. The "loss" here will be more than made up for in other areas.
